## Findings

The baseline true covariance SCNR was 13.515 dB at the 8 dB SINR target and 13.497 dB at the 12 dB target, from 32 draws per condition. The digitized 15.10 dB ordinate leaves gaps of about 1.58 and 1.60 dB. All 569 paired-control and initialization trajectories returned physically feasible streams. The final cohort contains 8,154 transmit SDPs with verified relative-or-absolute objective gaps within the registered gate.

- Covariance, full residual reconstruction and truncated streams scored equivalently at the registered resolution. Rank truncation had zero material effects on the 64 original AO draws under either kernel. AO updates and stops on covariance design SCNR. Its physical SINR and power checks use recovered streams after convergence. The priced LP accepts a recovered stream candidate using nominal physical feasibility and estimated-kernel SCNR with fixed U. It then refreshes U from the accepted streams and reports both kernel scores.
- The tested changes in AO iteration caps, inner and outer tolerances, channel distance draws, distance range, path-loss exponent and power cap each shifted the linear-averaged SCNR by less than 0.05 dB in both conditions. Increasing only the 100 W budget to 199.526 W with the original target coefficient shifted the paired medians by +0.051 and +0.049 dB. Multiplying only the target coefficient by 1.995262 shifts the score upward by 3 dB and overshoots the plotted values. The pinned figure script and baseline share the original coefficient and budget convention.
- The slow-time field change from 64 to 512 reproduced the exact endpoint scores and physical metrics on seed 13001 at both thresholds with fixed matrices. The distributed covariance file records 512 calibration snapshots. The design driver sets L=64, while the tutorial text describes L=32 secondary snapshots and a different probing alphabet. The original figure's exact calibration and Monte Carlo inputs are unavailable. That provenance gap prevents attributing the residual to a particular generation setting. The four-user dashed curve is distinct from the nearby eight-user dashed curve at 15.03 dB and the twelve-user dashed curve at 14.77 and 14.53 dB. [Author manuscript, Sec. VI-A](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10537v2)
- The 20-start runs on each of three draws gave a median AO spread of 0.434 dB for the design objective and 0.280 dB for true SCNR. The registered rule yields 0.867 dB and 0.561 dB tolerances, respectively. The existing 0.1 dB archival threshold remains part of the historical record.
- All 320 margin-study designs passed nominal feasibility. The intentional legacy tightening yielded a median achieved margin of about 0.000087 dB. The 1 dB setting cost about 0.0066 and 0.0059 dB in paired median true SCNR. At 1% relative RMS error, it retained joint feasibility for 38.5% and 10.6% of perturbations averaged over draws at the two thresholds. The fraction fell to 4.1% and 0.16% at 3% error. Across all settings, every design experienced at least one infeasible perturbation among the 100 repetitions at each tested error level.
